Danach bekommt FUSE Zugangsschlüssel („Tokens“) für Minecraft. Klappt die Anmeldung nicht (zum Beispiel, weil auf dem Konto kein Minecraft ist), fragt FUSE zusätzlich den Xbox-Gamertag ab, um ihn in der Fehlermeldung zu zeigen.

Die Dateien selbst schickt FUSE nicht.

So löschst du deine Daten

  • Anmeldung vergessen: In FUSE abmelden oder „Angemeldet bleiben“ ausschalten. Dann entfernt FUSE den gespeicherten Zugangsschlüssel.
  • FUSE ganz entfernen: Den FUSE-Ordner löschen, dann %APPDATA%\Fuse und %LOCALAPPDATA%\Fuse. Hast du einen anderen Spielordner eingestellt, lösche auch diesen.
    Achtung: In %APPDATA%\Fuse\mc liegen standardmäßig deine Welten. Sichere sie vorher.
  • Bei Microsoft: Unter https://account.live.com/consent/Manage kannst du Apps, denen du Zugriff auf dein Microsoft-Konto gegeben hast, den Zugriff wieder entziehen.

A few more details:

  • Modrinth: When you search, FUSE sends your search term and the selected Minecraft version to Modrinth, for mods also the loader. To show names and pictures of your installed content and to check mods for updates, FUSE sends the Modrinth identifiers (project IDs or slugs) of the content you installed through FUSE. When you check resource packs or shaders for updates, FUSE sends checksums (SHA-1 hashes, a kind of fingerprint of a file) of the files in the resource pack or shader folder, including files that did not come from Modrinth. FUSE does not send the files themselves.
  • With every connection, the service sees your IP address and technical details. The program identifier ("user agent") FUSE uses contains no information about you. Pictures shown in the FUSE window (e.g. mod icons, your skin) are loaded by WebView2 with its own browser identifier.
  • Loader projects: FUSE downloads from meta.fabricmc.net and maven.fabricmc.net (Fabric), meta.quiltmc.org and maven.quiltmc.org (Quilt), maven.minecraftforge.net (Forge) and maven.neoforged.net (NeoForge), plus the servers these projects specify for program parts the loader needs.
  • Minecraft itself: Once Minecraft is running, the game connects on its own to Mojang/Microsoft services and to the servers you play on. Their rules apply to that, not this notice.

How to delete your data

  • Forget a sign-in: Sign out in FUSE or turn off "Stay signed in". FUSE then removes the stored access key.
  • Remove FUSE completely: Delete the FUSE folder, then %APPDATA%\Fuse and %LOCALAPPDATA%\Fuse. If you set a different game folder, delete that one too.
    Careful: By default your worlds are in %APPDATA%\Fuse\mc. Back them up first.
  • At Microsoft: At https://account.live.com/consent/Manage you can remove access from apps you have allowed into your Microsoft account.
